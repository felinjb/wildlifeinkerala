        About us

        A new company, built around the forests of the south

        Wildlife in Kerala plans wildlife and nature routes across Kerala and the neighbouring parts of Tamil Nadu and Karnataka. Every route can be shortened, extended or mixed with another.

        We are new, so there are no reviews to show yet. Instead we put the facts up front: the nights at each stop, what is likely to be seen, and a plain answer when you ask about price, terms or sightings.

          Questions

          Good to know

          Will I see a tiger?

          Maybe, but probably not. Tigers are rare and shy, and no one can promise a sighting. Elephants, gaur, birds, tahr and monkeys are more likely on these routes. We plan enough time in the best parks to give you a fair chance.

          When is the best time to go?

          It depends on the park and the animals you want to see. Some parks close in the monsoon or during breeding seasons. Tell us your month and we will say what to expect.

          Can I change a route?

          Yes. Nights, stays and activities can be changed, and stops can be moved between routes.

          What does it cost?

          The price depends on group size, season, stays and vehicle. Message us with your dates and number of people and we reply with a quote.

          How do I book?

          Send an enquiry on WhatsApp or with the form below. We reply with a plan and a price. Nothing is charged until you confirm.
