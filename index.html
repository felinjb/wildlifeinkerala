    Questions

    Good to know

    When is the best time to go?

    Most of these parks are at their best from roughly October to May. It depends on the route and the animals you want to see, so tell us your dates and we will say what to expect.

    Can I change a route?

    Yes. Nights, stays and activities can be changed, and stops can be moved between routes.

    Will I see a tiger?

    Maybe. These are wild animals, and no operator can promise a sighting. We plan enough time in the best parks to give you a fair chance.

    How do I book?

    Send an enquiry with the form below or on WhatsApp. We reply with a plan and a price.
